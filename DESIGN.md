# Sodoh Nigeria Design System

## Direction

The site should feel like a clear route between Nigerian agricultural produce and the people who source it. Use the crop, market, and supply context to establish credibility; do not borrow the look of a generic climate-tech dashboard or imply proof the company has not supplied.

## Palette

- Brand green: `#016936`
- Fresh green: `#10B981`
- Light field: `#ECFDF5`
- Foreground: `#064E3B`
- Muted surface: `#E8F1F3`
- Border: `#A7F3D0`
- Destructive: `#DC2626`
- Dark surfaces: `#0D1B14` and `#14291E`; dark text: `#E7F4EB`

Use semantic CSS variables so light and dark themes retain the same hierarchy. Keep the public surface green-led and the admin surface mostly neutral.

## Type

Keep the existing Outfit display face and DM Sans body face. Outfit carries the brand voice in headings; DM Sans supports longer copy and controls. Use sentence case, balanced display headings, and a 65–75ch measure for body copy.

## Form and Material

- Public photography is the primary visual material. Use the existing real agriculture images and do not label stock photography as a Sodoh-owned farm or shipment.
- Public buttons, controls, and product media use a consistent 12–24px radius scale. Product images may use one distinct soft crop; do not add decorative frames around every section.
- Use open editorial layouts and simple dividers instead of repeated icon cards, numbered rows, or eyebrow labels.
- Admin follows Operate mode: predictable navigation, readable tables, compact forms, consistent 12px controls, and clear state feedback.

## Motion and Access

The only authored entrance is the hero photograph: a short, eased reveal that resolves from slight blur and crop to a clear image. Other sections render in place. Disable the reveal for reduced-motion preferences. Preserve keyboard focus, labels, status announcements, and WCAG AA contrast.

## Claims and Proof

Do not show seeded sample stats, testimonials, certifications, or team biographies as proof. Site copy and catalogue, FAQ, pillar, metric, and quote records stay out of the public view until a staff member marks them reviewed. Do not make environmental or supply-chain claims beyond approved, reviewable content. The live product catalogue and working enquiry paths are the available product demonstrations.
