# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business and individual customers looking to source Nigerian agricultural products; partners and stakeholders learning about the company; internal staff maintaining site content and handling enquiries.

## Product Purpose

The Sodoh Nigeria website introduces the company, presents its agricultural product catalogue, and gives visitors a direct way to discuss supply or partnership needs. Internal staff use its protected CMS to update public content and manage enquiries.

## Positioning

No distinctive operating mechanism is confirmed in the available product brief. Keep claims about sourcing, processing, certification, delivery, reach, and environmental impact factual and reviewable.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public routes: home, about, products, and contact.
- Visitors can submit contact messages and product quote requests.
- Staff can manage site content and enquiry records through the admin workspace.
- Staff can mark public copy and catalogue or proof records as reviewed before they appear publicly.
- Supabase remains the source for public content and admin data. Preserve existing fetching, auth, and mutation behavior.
- E-commerce, payments, and order management are future goals, not current capabilities.
- Preserve Server Components where present and isolate client interaction.

## Brand Commitments

Sodoh Nigeria is the product name. The redesign brief commits to the existing brand green, an organic biophilic visual language, grounded agricultural imagery, and clear support for light and dark themes. Use plain, active copy.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains agricultural stock photography and Supabase seed content. The seed includes unverified statistics, testimonials, certifications, team biographies, and environmental claims; do not present these as verified proof. No approved customer logos, certifications, or audited impact metrics were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Help buyers understand what is available and how to enquire.
- Keep the path from product discovery to a real conversation clear.
- Make public claims easy to review and update in the CMS.
- Do not imply e-commerce or capabilities that are not in the current product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, retain visible keyboard focus, support reduced motion, and provide responsive layouts and touch-friendly controls.
